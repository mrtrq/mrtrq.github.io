Hold them dearly
Hold them closely
Say what you want to say
As they will eventually go away

Rarely time is the enemy
Rarely time is the source of misery
It is always the thoughts that go astray
The thoughts that won’t go away

Why would you ignore the obvious?
While it is the most precious
Spend as you are very generous
Ask as you are very curious

Call it serendipity
No burden to carry
Know that you will have plenty
As you are patient with many

Rotate the bearing
Be firm on where you are heading
Keep the film rolling
You are directing

Striking lightning
Engine propelling
Fuel pumping
Adrenaline flowing

Help is coming
Leave no shortcoming
Then came the unveiling
The start of a new beginning

Time is a creation From The Most Merciful
Rejoice as you are safe forever, bound for no ending.
