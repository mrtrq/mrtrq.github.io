Recently, I am rebuilding a long lost activity that I used to enjoy: reading books. I realize now how precious it is to have the time and willingness to read. This state may not last forever, and this is me trying to make it count.

Moving towards a better state of mind is not going to be easy. Given the premise, I would like to start sharing the bits and lessons learned from the books I currently read or books I have read. I hope it can serve as an archive for myself, and a reference for others who would like to start or continue their reading journey.

One page may take you forward
One book can bring you forward
