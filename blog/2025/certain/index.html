If there’s one thing that I’m certain about myself, it will be the fact that I love campus and one day I aspire to go back and immerse myself again, hoping that it will remind me of the atmosphere, the joy, the spirit, the uncertainties, the opportunities, the adventures, the silly mistakes, the bold decisions, the carelessness, the seniors, the genuine acts, the juniors, the restlessness, the discussions, the lessons, the professors, the wisdoms.

I want to close this with a remark for the first-year or second-year undergrads who somehow managed to find this writing, please do make use of your time to do your adventures. It can be anything, really, anything. You just have to find a place to start, and willing to take the first step. Enjoy the ride.
