The adventures of Gon, Killua, Kurapika, and Leorio to be a Hunter is really entertaining and a peak storytelling. Every story arc has its own purposes and ultimately it leads into a beautiful & stil unsolvable puzzle.

My favorite story arc so far is the chimera ant. The buildup was executed to perfection and the climax is very rewarding for us the viewers. This arc involves a fair amount of moving parts but I always feel engaged for each one of them. There are two favorite moments that is worth to mention.

## Netero vs King of The Ant

This is the first time of watching how powerful Netero is, also a glimpse of his background, portraying his youth time where he practiced for nearly 5 years in the mountains, repeating the same movement sequence. This brutal repetition rewards him with a supreme level of power, speed, concentration, and mindfulness. I believe Netero is the best at the time to go face to face with Meruem, The Chimera Ant King. I don't want to spoil how things will unfold, but I will tell you that this arc is intense in its own sense.
